# Domestic Travel Reimbursement Policy
Policy ID: TRAVEL-2026
Version: 1.0

## FLIGHT-01 — Domestic Flight
- Economy class is required for domestic travel.
- Maximum reimbursable amount: INR 15,000 per trip.
- Receipt is mandatory.

## HOTEL-01 — Domestic Hotel
- Maximum reimbursable amount: INR 5,000 per night.
- Receipt is mandatory.

## MEAL-01 — Meals
- Maximum reimbursable amount: INR 1,500 per day.
- Receipt is mandatory when the claim amount exceeds INR 500.

## TAXI-01 — Taxi
- Maximum reimbursable amount: INR 2,000 per trip.
- Receipt is mandatory when the claim amount exceeds INR 500.

## GENERAL-01 — General Rules
- Unsupported expense categories are not eligible.
- Claims above INR 25,000 require manual Finance review.
- Missing mandatory supporting documents require manual review.
- Suspected duplicate claims require manual review.
- Ambiguous or conflicting policy interpretation requires manual review.
